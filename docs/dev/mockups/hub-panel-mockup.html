Policy: Critical water

    
    
criticaltag: water

    
    
Any recipient stops escalation. A dismissal counts as acknowledgement.

    
Step 1, at 0 min

    
Audience Everyone, channels banner, push, popup. Push level: time-sensitive.
Gate: time window 22:00 to 07:00, outcome downgrade (push passive, popup corner card).

    
Step 2, at 5 min

    
Audience Admins, channels push, speak. Push has an Acknowledge button.
Gate: acknowledgement, only if not acknowledged, otherwise skip.
Fallback: audience Everyone.

    
Sentence the panel shows

    
When a critical message tagged water appears, show it on banner, push and popup to Everyone (quieter at night). If nobody acknowledges within 5 minutes, push and speak to Admins.

    

    
Up to 4 steps. A second policy with the same severities and tags is refused.
