Three ways to call it

    
The same engine, called with more or less detail. You only add detail when you need more control.

    

      
Level 0: banner onlyToday's call, unchanged. No hub objects needed.
action: heads_up.create_banner
data:
  message: Window open

      
Level 1: channels + matched policyYou name channels and who; the policy matched by severity and tag adds the rest (here: escalate to Admins after 5 min).
action: heads_up.notify
data:
  message: Water leak
  severity: critical
  tag: water
  channels: [banner, push]
  audience: Everyone

      
Level 2: one-offNo policy, one speaker, own spoken text.
action: heads_up.notify
data:
  message: Doorbell
  tts_message: Someone is at the door
  channels: [speak]
  recipients: [Kitchen speaker]
  policy: none
