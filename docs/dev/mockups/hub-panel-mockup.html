Heads Up: hub panel (mockup)

Static mockup of the proposed admin panel. Not part of the integration; nothing is sent or saved.

Examples of calls, from the simplest to the most detailed, and answers to common questions. Names (Anna, Admins, Kitchen speaker) are samples.

Examples

1. Banner only, as today

No hub objects needed. Nothing else is sent.

action: heads_up.create_banner
data:
  message: Window open

2. Banner and push, policies add the rest

The policies that match severity and tag also apply, here an escalation to Admins after 5 minutes.

action: heads_up.notify
data:
  message: Water leak
  severity: critical
  tag: water
  channels: [banner, push]
  to:
    push: {audience: Everyone}

3. Push, speech and persistent notification together

Each channel has its own target. Persistent notification needs none.

action: heads_up.notify
data:
  message: Water leak
  tts_message: Water in the utility room
  severity: critical
  channels: [push, speak, persistent]
  to:
    push: {audience: Admins}
    speak: {recipients: [Ground floor speakers]}

4. One-off, no policy

Speech on one speaker, ignoring all policies.

action: heads_up.notify
data:
  message: Doorbell
  tts_message: Someone is at the door
  channels: [speak]
  to:
    speak: {recipients: [Kitchen speaker]}
  policy: none

5. Shortcut for one channel

The push shortcut with a presence audience: the person whose departure emptied the house.

action: heads_up.push
data:
  message: Window left open
  audience: Last to leave

6. To one person

action: heads_up.notify
data:
  message: Washer is done
  severity: info
  channels: [push]
  to:
    push: {recipients: [Anna]}

Questions

Do I need the hub for banners?

No. create_banner, clear_banner, clear_all, watch rules and the card work as today without any hub object.

What is the difference between audience and direct recipients?

An audience is a named group of people that can change over time (Everyone, At home, Last to leave). Direct recipients are fixed targets such as one person, one phone or one speaker. A channel takes one or the other, never both.

Why does speech take no audience?

An audience is a set of people, and speech goes to a speaker in a place. The hub does not know which speaker a person is near, so you name the speakers.

Can one call send push, speech and persistent notification?

Yes. Name the channels and give each channel its own target in "to", as in example 3.

What happens if nobody reacts?

An escalation policy can notify more people after a delay, and a reminder policy can repeat. Any acknowledgement stops both. Whether dismissing counts as acknowledging is a setting of the escalation policy.

How do I keep messages quiet at night?

Add a quiet hours policy: hold messages until the end, send them quietly, or drop them. Critical messages can pass anyway.

What if someone is not home?

An away policy sends the message when they arrive, while the message is still active. A fallback policy can send to someone else instead.

Can I get one summary instead of many messages?

Yes. A summarize policy holds matching messages and sends one summary every N minutes or at a fixed time, with the count and titles. While a message waits, nothing is sent individually on any channel (the dashboard banner too).

What happens when I replace a phone?

Edit the person's recipient profile once. Audiences and policies do not change, and no automation needs editing.

Why didn't I get a notification?

The Test send tab shows who would be notified for a given call and why anyone was skipped, held or dropped. The Trace tab shows what happened for a real message.

What happens after a restart?

Pending escalations keep their due times. A step that was due during the restart runs once if the message is still active and unacknowledged. Pushes of messages that no longer exist are cleared.
