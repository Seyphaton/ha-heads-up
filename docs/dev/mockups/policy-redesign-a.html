→ Always gets through

For these messages, quiet hours and summaries do not apply to the people covered. Presence still decides by where people are. Each entry only lets through; it sends nothing.

  • Critical critical covers everyone it reaches. Sent by Critical to Admins.get_through · switch.heads_up_policy_critical
  • Alarms wake Admins tag alarm covers only Admins. Sent by Alarms to Admins.get_through · switch.heads_up_policy_alarms_wake_admins
