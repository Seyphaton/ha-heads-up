Two views: Inbox and Everything

  • Inbox: messages sent to the inbox on purpose. They have a read state and make the badge count.
  • Everything: Inbox plus the copies Heads Up kept of messages you got on push, popup, banner or speech. Copies are history: never unread, never in the badge.
  • Copies exist only when "Keep a copy of everything" is on (toggle in the bar above). Without it the Everything view shows the same items as Inbox and a hint how to switch it on. The Message Log keeps no text, so there is nothing to show for older messages.
  • A copy shows how it reached you (Push, Banner, Speech) in place of the unread dot.

Messages to people (Phase 23, D-265)

  • The pen button (list header and medium C) opens New message: pick people, write, Send. Try it; the bar switch "Messages to people" is the admin setting.
  • The recipient gets a normal item from a person: avatar, name, text. It counts as unread and shows on the badges like any message.
  • The sender gets a copy in Sent: no unread dot, never in the badge, no "read by". The sender never learns if or when it was read.
  • You cannot pick a severity. A message is a normal one, so Quiet hours apply, and it cannot break through Do Not Disturb.
  • Send via: Inbox is always on (the message must be kept). Phone and Screens are optional; default is Inbox + Phone. A person who does not take a channel (their profile) is skipped on it and still gets the inbox item; the dialog says so. Try Opa: no phone, screens only. Speech and mail are not offered.
  • Limit 1000 characters, 5 messages a minute (try sending six).
  • Switch the setting off: the pen and the Sent tab disappear, items already received stay.

What happens when you open it

  1. Tap the badge: the dialog opens on Inbox. Nothing is read yet.
  2. Tap a row: it expands (text, picture, timeline), the dot goes, the badge drops by one.
  3. Critical and unacknowledged: red badge, "Needs action". Opening does not clear it; acknowledging does.
  4. Resolved while you were away: marked "Resolved", still unread until opened.
  5. Mark all read clears the dots. Delete removes one item for you only.
