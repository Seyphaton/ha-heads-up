Design notes

    
    
      
  • Your four cases are two dropdowns: "is not in" + Hold = hold until in; "is in" + Hold = hold until they leave; "is in" + Drop = drop when in; "is not in" + Drop = drop when out.
    • 
      
  • Speech has no person, so it uses "nobody of" or "somebody of" an audience. "Anyone is not in" would be ambiguous.
    • 
      
  • One zone per policy, shared by both rows. Two zones need two policies.
    • 
      
  • Unknown presence (unknown, unavailable) delivers at once, as Away does today.
    • 
      
  • A stored Away policy opens as the first example: push, popup and mail; recipient not in Home; hold; same wait and critical setting.
    • 
      
  • "Critical messages pass" sits under Which messages, next to the severities, on every policy that holds back (Quiet hours, Presence, Summarize). It is hidden when the chosen severities exclude critical. Summarize's "Include critical" becomes the same switch with the opposite meaning.
    • 
      
  • Guests: put "Only if input_boolean.guest_mode is off" on a speech policy; with guests the policy steps aside.
