# Heads up - Brand guidelines

Use the exact product name **Heads up** and the subtitle **Smart notifications for Home Assistant**.

## Locked approved composition

Preserve the symmetrical house with no roof overhang, the compact open bell loop, the rounded bell shoulders and base, and the rounded semicircular clapper. The later explicitly approved visual reference supersedes earlier defective geometry. Existing color styles remain sourced from the original archive; severity palettes are additions.

The Main, Warning and Critical house-and-bell composition is shared. Warning uses amber/orange with a triangle exclamation badge; Critical uses red with an octagonal exclamation badge. Badges are separate elements. Their approved inward placement must not be moved toward the tile edge or allowed to overlap the bell.

## Correct boundary and transparency

The actual boundary is the luminous rounded-square tile. Do not add an opaque surrounding rectangle or external halo. Retain antialiased transparent edges. Circular delivery must use the tile-inscribed circle without artificial exterior padding. Do not crop by the old larger canvas.

## Usage

Use approved PNGs or matching lossless WebP for the luminous identity when visual fidelity to the approved artwork is the priority. Reconstructed SVGs are separately supplied, with differences documented; they are not interchangeable pixel-identical originals.

Choose Transparent Light for compatible light backgrounds and Transparent Dark for compatible dark backgrounds. Confirm contrast in the actual host interface. Monochrome White requires an appropriately dark surface. Preserve the original named color values rather than substituting assumed black or white values based only on filenames.

At 16-32 pixels, pair Warning and Critical with severity text and an accessible name. Badge shape and punctuation are not sufficient alone at those sizes. Do not claim webmanifest maskable support: purpose is any.

## Banner

Use the supplied title, subtitle, cyan underline and original background arrangement. The updated banner changes only the icon region before resizing to 1280 x 640. Keep Warning and Critical out of the banner. The supplied original is retained separately.
